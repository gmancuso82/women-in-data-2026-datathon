# Research idea backlog

## Current lead: healthy-diet affordability

**Working question:** Which countries have made healthy diets more affordable, and what food-system patterns could help similar countries close the affordability gap?

**Why it is promising:** It connects nutrition, food security, affordability, and a policy-relevant economic story. We will only commit after checking the data coverage and variables.

## Backup hypothesis: nutrition trade-off

**Working question:** Where is enough food energy available but diet quality or micronutrient adequacy still weak?

**Why preserve it:** It may become the stronger question if the affordability measures are too sparse, too recent, or do not support a compelling comparison.

## Decision rule

We will choose a direction after a small data landscape scan: available variables, country coverage, time coverage, and whether the data supports an actionable recommendation.
