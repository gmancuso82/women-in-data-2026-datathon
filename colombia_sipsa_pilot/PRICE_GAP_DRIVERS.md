# What drives the Bogotá-Medellín basket difference?

For each food, we subtract the Medellín price from the Bogotá price. A positive number means the food cost more at Bogotá's Corabastos market on that sampled date.

## Largest contributors to a higher Bogotá basket total

| Sampled date | Largest driver | Second driver | Third driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 February 2025 | Aguacate: +2,138 COP/kg | Chócolo mazorca: +1,442 | Zanahoria: +1,155 |
| 14 May 2025 | Guayaba: +1,834 COP/kg | Ahuyama: +950 | Banano: +925 |
| 13 August 2025 | Zanahoria: +2,299 COP/kg | Aguacate: +1,363 | Ahuyama: +1,163 |
| 12 November 2025 | Chócolo mazorca: +1,933 COP/kg | Guayaba: +1,420 | Ahuyama: +950 |

## What this tells us

The gap is **not driven by one permanently expensive food**. The largest contributors rotate across dates:

- Aguacate is a major driver in February and August.
- Guayaba is a major driver in May and November.
- Chócolo mazorca is a major driver in February and November.
- Zanahoria is the largest driver in August.
- Ahuyama appears among the largest contributors in May, August, and November.

This is an early descriptive result, not proof of causation. It may reflect local supply, harvest timing, transport, quality differences, or market conditions. It does show why location- and date-specific observations are useful.
