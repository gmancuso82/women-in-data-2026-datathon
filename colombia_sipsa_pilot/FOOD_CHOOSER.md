# Choose the fresh-food basket

Every food below has a reported wholesale price in both pilot markets on all four selected dates. The prices are the **average across those four dates** and are shown only to help you see the range of choices. They are not yet a basket score.

## Proposed starter basket: 10 foods

This is a practical first basket because it includes four vegetables, four fruits, and two starchy staples. The foods are familiar, consistently available, and simple to explain on a presentation slide.

| Group | Food | Why include it? |

| Vegetables | Cebolla cabezona blanca | Common cooking base |
| Vegetables | Zanahoria | Vegetable variety |
| Vegetables | Tomate | Vegetable variety |
| Vegetables | Ahuyama | Vegetable variety |
| Fruit | Banano | Common, lower-cost fruit |
| Fruit | Naranja | Fruit variety |
| Fruit | Guayaba | Fruit variety |
| Fruit | Aguacate | Fruit variety; source of dietary fat |
| Tubers and plantains | Yuca | Root starch |

**Important limitation:** this is a consistent *fresh-food market basket*, not a nutritionally complete diet. SIPSA's daily workbook for this pilot does not include enough comparable protein foods, dairy, grains, or oils to build an official healthy-diet basket.

## How to decide

Choose foods based on three rules:

1. Keep the three available groups represented: vegetables, fruit, and tubers/plantains.
2. Keep the same foods for every market and date.
3. Choose a small set that a viewer recognizes and can understand quickly.

The selected basket is saved in `data/basket_definition.csv`. The next calculation adds one kilogram of each food for every market/date and compares the reference-basket total across markets and seasons.
